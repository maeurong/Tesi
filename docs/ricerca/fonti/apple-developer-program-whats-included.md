## Membership Details

Membership provides the tools, resources, and support you need to create and deliver software and services to users around the world on over 2 billion active Apple devices.

## Software and tools

### Beta OS and SDK releases

Ensure your apps are ready for customers when platform releases become publicly available by testing with the latest beta versions of operating systems and SDKs.

- iOS
- iPadOS
- macOS
- tvOS
- visionOS
- watchOS

### Tools

Gain access to features that let you configure app services, manage your development teams, and submit new apps and updates.

#### Xcode

The Xcode integrated development environment (IDE) includes the tools you need to build and upload apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Vision Pro, and Apple Watch.

#### Xcode Cloud

Accelerate the development and delivery of your apps with a continuous integration and delivery service built into Xcode. Membership includes 25 compute hours/month.

#### Reality Composer Pro

Quickly prototype and produce content for spatial experiences.

#### Create ML

Build, train, and deploy machine learning models with no machine learning expertise required.

And more...

## Services and capabilities

Build your apps with a comprehensive set of services and capabilities that let you deliver advanced features to your users. Learn to leverage the power of integrated Apple hardware, software, and services to build seamless, intuitive, and multi-faceted experiences.

#### AirPlay

Let users wirelessly stream content from their iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

#### CloudKit

Store your app’s data in iCloud and keep everything up to date across apps and on the web. Membership includes up to 1PB of free storage for each app.

#### Core ML

Integrate a broad variety of machine learning model types into your app.

#### DeviceCheck

Reduce fraudulent use of your services by managing device state and asserting app integrity.

#### Face ID and Touch ID

Let users access your app content easily and securely.

#### Foundations Model framework

Access Apple’s on-device large language model that powers Apple Intelligence.

#### Handoff

Provide continuity for users with multiple devices by supporting Handoff in your apps and websites.

#### HealthKit

Deliver informed health and fitness solutions.

#### HomeKit and Matter

Allow users to communicate with and control connected accessories in their home.

## Identity verification

Enroll as an individual or an organization in more than 220 countries or regions and receive an Apple-verified Developer ID.

#### Individual and organization verification

To help establish a trusted global developer community, each Apple Developer Program membership is verified by Apple.

#### Team management

Invite members to your development team and assign specific roles.

#### Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles

Generate the certificates and profiles required to sign your apps and enable features like push notifications, keychain access, and more.

## Eligibility for distribution options

#### App Store

The App Store makes it easy for over a billion customers on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch and Apple Vision Pro to discover and download your apps, games, and sticker packs. And with Apple Arcade on the App Store, you can be a part of a game subscription service unlike any other\*.

#### Alternative app distribution

In certain regions, developers can use alternative distribution options, such as distributing apps outside the App Store, providing alternative payment options on the App Store, or operating alternative app marketplaces.

## Activities and events

Apple Developer Program members gain access to exclusive year-round activities, expert guidance, and interactive experiences that take place online and in person all over the world. Activities offer valuable advice for developers of all levels. Whether you’re just getting started, enhancing your existing app or game, refining your design, or working with a new platform, there’s something for you.

#### Meet with Apple

Explore new and innovative ways to design and develop great apps that take advantage of the latest Apple frameworks and technologies.

- **Sessions** showcase the latest in Apple tools, technologies, features, and best practices.
- **Appointments** are one-on-one meetings with Apple experts where you can get guidance on your app or game.
- **Labs** provide hands-on opportunities to test and optimize your apps for Apple platforms.
- **Workshops** are interactive experiences that allow you to dive into new features and technologies in small group settings.

**Special events** include a variety of activities, like WWDC and others, designed to inspire, guide, and connect the Apple developer community.

## Support

#### Code-level support requests

Ask Apple engineers to help troubleshoot your code or provide solutions that will accelerate your development.

#### Apple Developer Forums

Visit the Apple Developer Forums to post questions, exchange knowledge, and connect with Apple engineers and the developer community. Share TestFlight links for your app with other Apple Developer Program members to engage with new testers.

#### Bug reporting and feedback

Get more in-depth feedback about the status of your bug report. Gain visibility into feedback submitted across your entire team.

#### Account and enrollment support

Access comprehensive support 24/7 for any of your apps across Apple platforms. Support is offered via email and phone in 9 languages.

#### Pricing and fees

The Apple Developer Program is 99 USD per membership year, or in local currency where available.<sup>1</sup>

The commission on the sale of digital goods and services through the App Store is 30% (15% if you’re enrolled in the [App Store Small Business Program](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/), [Video Partner Program](https://developer.apple.com/programs/video-partner/), [Mini Apps Partner Program](https://developer.apple.com/programs/mini-apps-partner/), or [News Partner Program](https://developer.apple.com/apple-news/program/)) and 15% for qualifying subscriptions.<sup>2</sup>
